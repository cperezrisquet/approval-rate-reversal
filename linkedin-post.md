never a link, never a screenshot. LinkedIn renders it as a swipeable
carousel and the first page is the feed thumbnail.

Post body target: ~2,400 characters. LinkedIn truncates at ~3,000 and
collapses everything after the third line behind "…see more", so the first
three lines carry the whole burden.

**No Markdown** in the body — LinkedIn renders asterisks and underscores
literally. **No Unicode bold** either: it breaks screen readers.

## Document title

A LinkedIn post has no title field — only a body. When a PDF is attached,
LinkedIn asks for a **document title**, shown in bold under the carousel.
Blank falls back to the filename. It is a second hook, read by people who
look at the document before the text, so it has to stand alone.

Maximum **58 characters**. Lead with the figures, and carry the finding that
appears nowhere else in the feed.
